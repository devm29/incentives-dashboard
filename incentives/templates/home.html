What the chart shows

Each line is one UID's incentive over the reported window. A subnet can hold up to 256 UIDs, so the chart plots only the highest-peaking ones and states how many were left out — a legend with 256 entries is not a legend. Observations that sit far outside their own series' behaviour are ringed and listed beneath the chart.
